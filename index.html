How the preview works

The estimate follows the shared recharge schedule, separately for every character.

The target is the highest 15-energy step that does not exceed 840. For example, a character at 815 reaches a preview target of 830 on the next tick; it won’t predict 845.

Names, energy values, and the schedule save automatically in local browser storage.
